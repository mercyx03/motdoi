Implementation limits: contemporary context, not a historical policy timeline. Areas without sufficiently verified female ordination policy remain marked as needing verification, not described as prohibiting women. Same-sex married clergy receive automatic eligibility only in the modeled US and Scottish cases; elsewhere the message calls for local verification. This is a simulation coverage limit, not a claim of universal prohibition. Sexual orientation alone is never a rejection criterion. The model does not assign moral traits to worship styles, countries, gender or orientation.

The three-year course, one-year diaconate, eight-year priest service, age 35, ten community-service years and acceptance probabilities are game design values, not canonical rules. Ministry is a recorded community role, not a replacement salaried career; no salary or donated funds are minted. A candidate can remain married and retain ordinary work. Transferring requires acceptance and never resets education into an automatic promotion.
