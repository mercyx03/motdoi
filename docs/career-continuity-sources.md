The October 22 update uses these official references for names and the distinction between civilian appointments and military/police ranks:

- [Law 72/2025/QH15, local government](https://xaydungchinhsach.chinhphu.vn/toan-van-luat-so-72-2025-qh15-to-chuc-chinh-quyen-dia-phuong-119250618161434371.htm): the local-government ladder uses commune and province levels, rather than adding new district offices.
- [Commune chair and deputy-chair responsibilities](https://xaydungchinhsach.chinhphu.vn/huong-dan-ky-nang-thuc-hien-nhiem-vu-quyen-han-cua-chu-tich-pho-chu-tich-ubnd-cap-xa-119250811085745609.htm).
- [Senior military and police rank promotions](https://baochinhphu.vn/bon-si-quan-cap-cao-quan-doi-va-cong-an-duoc-thang-quan-ham-cap-bac-ham-thuong-tuong-102250801180003654.htm): the existing ladder now continues through lieutenant general, senior lieutenant general and general.

Pay, chances of appointment, time between appointments and service extensions are game balancing choices, not a legal or salary guide. A senior appointment is a separate prerequisite for further general-rank advancement. Characters and scandals are fictional; allegations do not create a criminal conviction. Adult casual relationships require both participants to be at least 18, and permit refusal without a forced relationship.

Scholarships now store the destination, temporary residence, stipend and defense attempt together. Existing saves are repaired only when their research job and scholarship log provide evidence of the missing move; an ordinary doctorate alone is never used to infer travel.
