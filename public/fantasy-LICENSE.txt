FANTASY DATA ATTRIBUTION

The Fantasy mode includes semantic Vietnamese adaptations of the following
datasets, whose dataset cards declare the MIT license:

1. limloop / Fantasy Creature Bestiary
   https://huggingface.co/datasets/limloop/fantasy_creature_bestiary
2. ItsHotdogFred / GregAI NPC Training Data v1
   https://huggingface.co/datasets/ItsHotdogFred/npc-training-data-v1
3. Angry-Wizard / RPG Grid Maps (map_info.csv metadata only; no map images)
   https://huggingface.co/datasets/Angry-Wizard/rpg_grid_maps
4. Marcylene / RPG AI (five Portuguese quest descriptions)
   https://huggingface.co/datasets/Marcylene/rpg_ai
5. practical-dreamer / RPGPT PublicDomain (v2 alpaca)
   https://huggingface.co/datasets/practical-dreamer/RPGPT_PublicDomain-alpaca

Copyright remains with the respective dataset creators and contributors.
Dataset cards and checksums are retained in docs/fantasy-sources in the source
repository. Vietnamese names and descriptions are semantic adaptations of
structured attributes. Long dialogues are reduced to thematic motifs and
re-authored in this game's own setting; they are not represented as verbatim
translations. Unrelated mature-content motifs are filtered. The realm, houses,
skills, combat, economy and life simulation are original game design.
